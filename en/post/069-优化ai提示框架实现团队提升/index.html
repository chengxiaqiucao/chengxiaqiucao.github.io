DeepSeek Prompt Frameworks Help Teams Improve the Status Quo and Boost Efficiency

Building a Prompt Framework

Mar 19, 2025
3 minute read
中文

With the rapid development of AI technology, from ChatGPT to the explosion of DeepSeek, AI can now genuinely play a role in assisting our daily work.

Beyond specific areas such as assisted programming and copywriting, we can also make full use of AI’s powerful logical capabilities and vast foundational data to help us propose practical and feasible improvement plans for some of the difficulties we encounter at work.

But for AI to give us actionable solutions, the design of the prompt framework and the contextual information provided to AI are extremely critical. A well-designed prompt framework can help us quickly obtain highly valuable references.

A Case of the Predicament We Face

Here we use a fairly common case of an agile team’s quality predicament to illustrate:

As a test engineer responsible for quality assurance in an agile team running the Scrum framework, the team also includes an SM, a PO, two Android developers (among whom Xiao Li is responsible for the overall system design of the App), one back-end developer, and one part-time visual designer. The team’s product is a companion Android App for an IoT smart bracelet product. However, due to issues such as coordination with the hardware team, frequently changing requirements, untimely visual design, delayed test submissions, and a backlog of bugs, product quality is not optimistic. The App has now gone through multiple iterations, and the planned launch date is drawing ever closer. How can we propose an improvement plan for the current quality predicament?

Designing the Prompt Framework

For AI to help us produce a feasible improvement plan, we first need to design the prompt framework reasonably, so that the final plan better fits the team’s current situation and is actionable.

The key points of the prompt framework design are then:

  • A given role, so that AI determines from what perspective to propose the improvement plan
  • The current status quo, that is, sufficient background information and context
  • What AI should analyze in detail, or what its task should be
  • The specific output expected from AI, including format requirements and the elements to be included
  • Some relevant constraints, which have an important impact on whether the plan is feasible

Now, for the predicament above, I want AI to help me provide an overall quality improvement plan for the team that can be discussed at the retrospective meeting. Based on the above thinking, by inputting the prompt framework into DeepSeek, we get the following output:

As we can see, we have obtained an improvement plan that is highly targeted and quite feasible. Based on the team’s current situation, we can actually put it into implementation after some fine-tuning.

So making good use of AI prompt frameworks can not only help us get assistance on specific tasks, but also provide tremendous support for overall improvement initiatives of this kind.
